# Hero Copy — Homepage & Creator Landing

**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Context:** `.agents/product-marketing.md` v1
**Status:** Proposed — not implemented. Nothing on the live site has been changed.

Two hard constraints applied throughout:
- The `BetaNotice` component stays exactly as it is, wherever it already appears.
- No payout, earnings or subscription is described as operating. Terms may be stated as terms.

---

## Part 1 — Homepage hero (`src/components/ViewerHome.tsx`)

### The actual problem with this hero

It is a Netflix hero. The `<h1>` is the featured film's title, the paragraph is that film's
synopsis, and the buttons are Watch Now / Trailer. That works for Netflix because nobody
arrives at netflix.com wondering what Netflix is.

YouMakeTV is a new category. A first-time visitor lands on a film they have never heard of,
made by a studio they have never heard of, in a format they may not know exists — and the
page never tells them what this site is. The single most valuable sentence on the homepage
is missing.

**Recommendation:** keep the cinematic film hero. Do not replace it with a SaaS-style value
proposition — that would trade away the one thing the page does well. Instead add one
positioning line above the film title, inside the existing hero, where the "Featured Film"
pill currently sits.

### Recommended

Replace the `Featured Film` eyebrow pill with a positioning line, and keep everything below
it untouched:

> **Eyebrow (replaces the pill):**
> Every film here was made with AI.

> **Then, unchanged:** film title (h1), synopsis, Watch Now / Trailer.

Add one line beneath the CTA row, small and quiet:

> 20 of the 100 films are free to watch.

**Why:** it answers "what is this?" and "what does it cost me to find out?" without displacing
the film. A free-film count is the strongest thing this product can say to a stranger.

**Corrected 2026-08-21:** an earlier draft of this deck said *"Most of the catalog is free to
watch."* That is **false**. The catalog is 100 films: exactly 20 are priced at 0, and the other
80 split evenly across $0.99 / $2.99 / $4.99 / $7.99. The claim was caught in review before it
shipped. Any copy quoting this number must be re-counted against `src/data/movies.ts` whenever
the catalog changes — and if the free count stops being a round, impressive number, drop the
line rather than fudge it.

### Eyebrow alternatives

- **Option A — "Every film here was made with AI."**
  Plain, declarative, unmissable. Treats AI as the fact rather than the pitch. Best for a
  visitor who has no idea what the site is.
- **Option B — "AI-made films. The whole catalog."**
  Tighter and more confident. Slightly more insider — assumes the visitor already finds the
  idea interesting.
- **Option C — "Featured film · Made with AI"**
  Most conservative. Keeps the existing pill pattern and just qualifies it. Lowest risk,
  lowest impact.

### Supporting line alternatives

- **Option A — "20 of the 100 films are free to watch."** Concrete, checkable, removes risk.
- **Option B — "Free to start watching. No subscription needed."** Adds the second objection
  (do I have to subscribe?) but is longer.
- **Option C — omit.** If the hero feels crowded, the eyebrow alone does most of the work.

### What NOT to add

No viewer counts, no "join thousands of viewers," no "trusted by creators." There are no
such numbers. The dead `HeroSection.tsx` component contains the line *"{viewerCount} viewers
are browsing AI Cinema now"* — that is fabricated social proof. The component is unused; it
should be deleted rather than revived.

### Meta (for reference — current tags are already good)

- **Title:** YouMakeTV.ai | Watch and Publish AI-Generated Movies *(unchanged — it works)*
- **Meta description:** Watch AI-generated films from independent creators. 20 films free to
  watch. Publish your own AI film and set your own price.

## Part 2 — Creator landing hero (`src/components/CreatorPortal.tsx`)

### The honesty problem, first

Three things on this page state or imply that payouts are operating. They are not.

| Location | Current copy | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Hero stat strip | **"Monthly"** / "Payout schedule" | States a payout schedule as fact. No payout has ever been sent. |
| FAQ, "How do payouts work?" | "...Earnings are tracked inside the creator portal and **paid out monthly**." | Direct promise of monthly payment. |
| Creator Spotlight | "AI Noir Studio — 12 films, **earned $1,240**, Top Earner" | Invented creators with invented earnings, presented as real people. This is the most serious item on the page. |
| Dashboard preview | "$3,420 revenue", "Pending payout **$398.40** — Processing — Paid Jul 1, 2026" | Not labelled as illustrative. Reads as a real account. |

This page is also the only creator-facing surface with **no** `BetaNotice`. The notice
appears on the dashboard, the demo portal and onboarding — every place a creator sees money
figures *after* signing up — but not on the page that convinces them to sign up. That is
backwards: the disclosure arrives after the decision.

**Recommendation:** the Creator Spotlight section should be deleted outright, not reworded.
There is no honest version of a fake testimonial. The rest can be fixed with copy.

### Recommended hero

> **Eyebrow:** Creators
>
> **Headline:**
> Your AI film deserves
> **a real release.**
>
> **Subhead:**
> Publish AI-generated films to an audience that came looking for exactly that. Set your
> own price, keep your rights, and build a studio page that belongs to you.
>
> **Stat strip:**
> | Up to 40% | Free | You keep it |
> | Revenue share on paid views | To join and publish | Full ownership of your work |
>
> **Beta line, directly under the CTA:**
> Creator beta — uploads and studio pages are live. Billing is not connected yet, so no
> payouts have been made. We will email every creator before real payments begin.

**Why the headline changed.** "Turn AI Movies Into Income." is a promise the platform cannot
currently keep — it leads with the one thing that does not work. It is also the most generic
line available; every creator platform since 2010 has said a version of it.

"Your AI film deserves a real release" leads with what the platform *does* deliver today:
distribution, a storefront, and an audience with matching intent. It carries the emotional
charge the context doc identifies — creators feel their work is dismissed — and answers it.
"Deserves" is doing real work: it takes the creator's side against the platforms that bury
them.

**Why the stat strip changed.** Two of the three stats stay. "Monthly / Payout schedule"
becomes "You keep it / Full ownership of your work" — a benefit that is true today, is a
genuine differentiator, and directly answers the top creator objection. The strip loses
nothing and stops lying.

### Headline alternatives

- **Option A — "Your AI film deserves a real release."**
  Leads with dignity and distribution. Truthful today. Recommended.
- **Option B — "Built for the films everywhere else buries."**
  Sharper, more oppositional. Names the enemy (YouTube's treatment of AI work) without
  naming names. Strong for a creator audience that feels this daily; slightly risky as a
  first impression.
- **Option C — "Release your AI film. Set your price. Keep your rights."**
  Three concrete promises, all currently true, no interpretation required. Least memorable,
  most bulletproof.

### Subhead alternatives

- **Option A (recommended)** — as above. Covers audience intent, pricing control, ownership.
- **Option B** — "The only catalog where every film is AI-made — so nobody has to be
  convinced your work belongs." Sharper on differentiation, weaker on the practical.

### CTA

Current: "Start Free" / "View Demo Dashboard".

- **Primary:** **Publish Your First Film** — names the outcome, not the paperwork.
  Alternative: "Create Your Studio" if the studio page is the stronger hook.
- **Secondary:** **See the Creator Dashboard** — keep, but the destination must be labelled
  as a demo with sample data (it currently is, at `/creator/demo`).

### FAQ rewrite — "How do payouts work?"

> **Current:** "Creators earn a share of revenue generated from paid views and purchases.
> Earnings are tracked inside the creator portal and paid out monthly."

> **Replace with:** "Revenue share is 30% of paid views, rising to 40% for the Pro tier.
> Billing is not connected yet — no viewer has been charged and no payout has been sent, so
> any figures in the creator portal are simulated. We will email every creator before real
> payments begin, and publish the payout schedule then."

**Why:** it keeps the commercial terms (which are real and attractive) and removes the
timing promise (which is not). A creator who reads this and signs up anyway is a creator who
will not feel misled later.

### Other lines on the page needing the same treatment

- **Benefit card, "No subscribers required":** *"Start earning from day one."* → *"No
  follower count required. Publish your first film the day you join."*
- **Benefit card, "Earn recurring revenue":** *"Every new view on existing films generates
  income."* → *"Revenue share applies to every paid view, including on films you published
  months ago."* (States the term, not the income.)
- **Section heading, "Creators Are Earning. Track Everything.":** → **"Everything you
  publish, measured."** The current heading asserts creators are earning. None are.
- **Dashboard preview:** add a visible label — *"Sample data — illustrative dashboard"* —
  on the preview card itself, not in a footnote.
